## Q4 — `math2-q04-shaded-inequality.svg` (page 71)

Solid line on a square xy-plane with the half-plane to the right/above shaded.

- **viewBox** `0 0 440 440`; padding 44, so the plot box is 352 × 352
- **Plot extent**: x from −8 to 8, y from −8 to 8; **22 px per unit** in both directions
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8; italic `O` just
  below-left of the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Shading**: `#bfbfbf` at 42% opacity over `4x + y ≥ 16`, drawn *under* the grid
  so the gridlines stay visible inside it
- **Boundary line**: 2.6 px solid black, `y = −4x + 16`, from (2, 8) to (6, −8)
- **No marked points** in the original
- **Answer note**: the shaded side is `4x + y ≥ 16`. Of the four axis intercepts
  (±5, 0) and (0, ±5), only (5, 0) satisfies it (**D**)

## Q12 — `math2-q12-line-graph.svg` (page 79)

Single steep line on a square xy-plane. Same item-family as 2025-march-int-c Q10.

- **viewBox** `0 0 488 488`; padding 44, so the plot box is 400 × 400
- **Plot extent**: x from −10 to 10, y from −10 to 10; **20 px per unit** in both directions
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; italic `O` just
  below-left of the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Line**: 2.6 px black, `y = 6x + 9`, drawn edge-to-edge from (−19/6, −10) to (1/6, 10)
- **Marked points**: filled dots at `(0, 9)` and `(−2, −3)`
- **Answer note**: `y = 6x + 9` ⇔ `12x − 2y = −18`, i.e. `12x − Ry = −18` with R = 2 (**D**)

## Q13 — `math2-q13-shaded-half-plane.svg` (page 80)

Shallow boundary line low on the grid with everything above it shaded.

- **viewBox** `0 0 502 475`; padding 44
- **Plot extent**: x from −10 to 1, y from −15 to 0; **37.6 px per x-unit, 25.8 px per y-unit**
  (the original grid cells are wider than they are tall — this is reproduced)
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **Shading**: `#bfbfbf` at 42% opacity over the whole region above the line, drawn *under*
  the grid so the gridlines stay visible inside it; the shading spans the full plot box,
  including the narrow strip right of the y-axis
- **Boundary line**: 2.6 px solid black (the inequality is non-strict), `y = −x/3 − 12`,
  from (−10, −8.667) to (1, −12.333)
- **Marked points**: filled dots at `(0, −12)` and `(−6, −10)`
- **x-axis**: forms the top edge of the plot; ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2 sit
  *below* the axis, over the shading; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right; italic `O`
  below-left of the origin
- **y-axis**: ticks at every 2 units with labels −2, −4, −6, −8, −10, −12, −14 placed to the
  **right** of the axis (the left side is shaded in the original); arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Answer note**: `y = −x/3 − 12` ⇔ `x + 3y = −36`, and (0, 0) satisfies `≥`, so the shaded
  side is correct → r = 1, t = 3, r + t = 4 (**A**)

## Q15 — `math2-q15-exponential-graph.svg` (page 82)

Reflected exponential falling steeply just past x = 1. Same item-family as
2025-march-int-c Q16 (base 3) and 2025-march-int-a Q17 (base 5).

- **viewBox** `0 0 460 516`; padding 44
- **Plot extent**: x from −1 to 5, y from −5 to 10; **62 px per x-unit, 28.5 px per y-unit**
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; italic `O` below-left of the origin;
  arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Curve**: 2.6 px black graph of `y = −6ˣ + 5`, sampled every 0.02 in x
  - enters at the left edge at `(−1, 4.833)`, nearly flat under the asymptote `y = 5`
  - passes through `(0, 4)`
  - passes through `(1, −1)`
  - clipped where it leaves the bottom of the plot at `x = log₆10 ≈ 1.285`
  - no asymptote line is drawn and no points are dotted in the original
- **Answer note**: graphed curve is `y = f(x) + 2 = −6ˣ + 5` → `f(x) = −6ˣ + 3` (**B**)

## Verification / transcription notes

- The PDF contains **no math answer key**, so every `correctAnswer` in `_math2.json`
  was solved from the item itself and carries a `showWork` field with the derivation.
- Page map used (question → PDF page): 1→68, 2→69, …, 22→89 (`page = 67 + Q`).
  There are no scrolled second views in this module.
- Every item was transcribed from its `page-NN.png`. OCR of the watermarked
  screens was used as a cross-check, not as the source of record.
- **Cross-form check**: `2025-march-int-c` is a parallel form of the same paper.
  Its Math M2 Q10 is this form's Q12 (same `y = 6x + 9` line, key D); its Q12 is
  this form's Q13 with −77 in place of −36 (key 8 vs 4); its Q16 is this form's
  Q15 with base 3 in place of 6 (key B, same position in the family); its Q20–Q22
  are the same item types with different constants. Derivations agree in structure.
