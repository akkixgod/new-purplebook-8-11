## Q2 — `math1-q02-scatterplot.svg` (page 56)

- Axes: x from 0 to 8 (ticks/labels every 1), y from 0 to 16 (gridlines every 1,
  labels every 2). Italic `x` / `y` axis letters, italic `O` at the origin.
- Data points (10 filled dots, read off the source grid):
  (0.5, 2), (1.6, 4), (2.5, 4), (2.5, 6), (3.6, 5), (3.6, 8), (4.4, 8), (4.4, 9),
  (5.5, 12), (6.7, 12)
- Line of best fit drawn as **y = 0.8 + 1.7x** from x = 0 to x ≈ 8.4 (matches the
  drawn line, which leaves the top-right of the plot near y ≈ 15).
- **Answer note:** the drawn line rises and has a positive y-intercept, so the key is
  **A** (`y = 0.8 + 1.7x`); B/D have a negative slope, C a negative intercept.

- Grid covers x from −14 to 0 and y from −14 to 0 (unit gridlines). The x-axis runs

## Q12 — `math1-q12-transversal-angles.svg` (pages 67 + 68)

- Two vertical lines labeled **r** (left) and **s** (right), italic labels above.
- Transversal **k** running from upper-left to lower-right, italic `k` at its top end.
- At line r: `w°` upper-right of the intersection, `x°` lower-right.
  At line s: `y°` upper-right, `z°` lower-right. (So w and y are corresponding
  angles — that is the whole point of the item.)
- Caption centered below: *Note: Figure not drawn to scale.*
- No numeric angle values on the figure; 146 appears only in the stem.

## Q18 — `math1-q18-exponential-graph.svg` (pages 75 + 76)

- Grid from −10 to 10 in x and −10 to 6 in y, gridlines every 1, labels every 2,
  arrowed axes, italic `x` / `y`, `O` at the origin.
- Curve drawn as **y = −0.6(4.7)^x − 5**: flat along the horizontal asymptote
  y = −5 for x ≲ −2, bending down through about (0, −5.6) and plunging off the
  bottom of the grid near x ≈ 1.4. Curve never crosses the x-axis.
- **Answer note:** ab^x is negative for all x and c = −5, so f(x) < −5 always → the
  graph has no x-intercept → **D (Zero)**.

  the triangle just left of **C**.
- No side lengths on the figure (36√3 appears only in the stem).
- Caption centered below: *Note: Figure not drawn to scale.*
