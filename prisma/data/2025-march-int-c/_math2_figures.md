SVGs live under `public/mocks/2025-march-int-c/figures/`.
All three are generated by `scripts/_gen_math2_figs_marchintc.py`.
Style: clean black-on-white College Board look (1–2.6 px strokes, Georgia serif,
italic axis labels, no colour, no watermark).

Every geometry figure below was measured off the page PNG by pixel-tracing the
axes (to fix the origin and the pixels-per-unit in each direction) and then the
plotted line/curve, so the extents, tick labels and marked points reproduce the
original screens rather than being eyeballed.

## Q10 — `math2-q10-line-graph.svg` (page 90)

Single steep line on a square xy-plane.

- **viewBox** `0 0 488 488`; padding 44, so the plot box is 400 × 400
- **Plot extent**: x from −10 to 10, y from −10 to 10; **20 px per unit** in both directions
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; italic `O` just
  below-left of the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 (left of the axis);
  arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Line**: 2.6 px black, `y = 6x + 9`, drawn edge-to-edge from (−19/6, −10) to (1/6, 10)
- **Marked points**: filled dots at `(0, 9)` and `(−2, −3)` (both are dotted in the original)
- **Measured calibration**: origin at pixel (980, 524), 20.5 px per unit; the traced line gives
  x = 0.02 at y = 8.98 and x = −3.07 at y = −9.56 → slope 6.00, y-intercept 9, x-intercept −1.5
- **Answer note**: `y = 6x + 9` ⇔ `12x − 2y = −18`, i.e. `12x − Ry = −18` with R = 2 (**D**)

## Q12 — `math2-q12-shaded-half-plane.svg` (pages 92 + 93)

Shallow boundary line low on the grid with everything above it shaded.

- **viewBox** `0 0 502 475`; padding 44
- **Plot extent**: x from −10 to 1, y from −15 to 0; **37.6 px per x-unit, 25.8 px per y-unit**
  (the original grid cells are wider than they are tall — this is reproduced)
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **Shading**: `#bfbfbf` at 42% opacity over the whole region above the line, drawn *under*
  the grid so the gridlines stay visible inside it (as in the original); the shading spans the
  full plot box, including the narrow strip right of the y-axis
- **Boundary line**: 2.6 px solid black (the inequality is non-strict), `y = −x/7 − 11`,
  from (−10, −9.571) to (1, −11.143)
- **Marked points**: filled dots at `(0, −11)` and `(−7, −10)`
- **x-axis**: forms the top edge of the plot; ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2 sit
  *below* the axis, over the shading; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right; italic `O`
  below-left of the origin
- **y-axis**: ticks at every 2 units with labels −2, −4, −6, −8, −10, −12, −14 placed to the
  **right** of the axis (the left side is shaded in the original); arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Measured calibration**: origin at pixel (1156, 159), 37.6 px per x-unit and 25.8 px per
  y-unit; the traced line gives y = −9.57 at x = −9.87 and y = −11.03 at x = 0.24 → slope
  −0.145 ≈ −1/7, y-intercept −11
- **Answer note**: `y = −x/7 − 11` ⇔ `x + 7y = −77`, and (0, 0) satisfies `≥`, so the shaded
  side is correct → r = 1, t = 7, r + t = 8 (**A**)

## Q16 — `math2-q16-exponential-graph.svg` (pages 97 + 98)

Reflected exponential falling steeply just past x = 1.

- **viewBox** `0 0 460 516`; padding 44
- **Plot extent**: x from −1 to 5, y from −5 to 10; **62 px per x-unit, 28.5 px per y-unit**
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; italic `O` below-left of the origin;
  arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Curve**: 2.6 px black graph of `y = −3ˣ + 5`, sampled every 0.02 in x
  - enters at the left edge at `(−1, 4.667)`, nearly flat under the asymptote `y = 5`
  - passes through `(0, 4)`
  - crosses the x-axis at `x = log₃5 ≈ 1.465`
  - clipped where it leaves the bottom of the plot at `x = log₃10 ≈ 2.096`
  - no asymptote line is drawn and no points are dotted in the original
- **Measured calibration**: origin at pixel (844.5, 460), 65.9 px per x-unit and 28.8 px per
  y-unit; traced samples 4.72 at x = −1.131, 4.00 at x = 0, 2.05 at x = 0.994, 0.10 at
  x = 1.449 — all within 0.02 of `−3ˣ + 5`
- **Answer note**: graphed curve is `y = f(x) + 2 = −3ˣ + 5` → `f(x) = −3ˣ + 3` (**B**)

### Gap: Q10 choices C and D are not in the PDF

The page-90 capture is cut off immediately below choice B, and unlike Q12/Q16 there is **no
scrolled second view** of the Q10 screen anywhere in the PDF (the OCR for page 90 likewise
recovers only `Rx + 12y = 18`). Only these are directly legible:

```
A) Rx + 12y = 18
B) Rx − 12y = −18
```

Choices C and D in `_math2.json` are **reconstructed** as `12x + Ry = 18` and `12x − Ry = −18`,
the standard College Board pairing for this item (the 2 × 2 grid over "which coefficient is the
unknown constant" × sign). The item carries `_reconstructedChoices: ["C", "D"]` and a `note`.

The key is not in doubt: the graphed line is `y = 6x + 9`, whose y-intercept is 9, and neither
visible choice can produce that intercept (both force ±18/12 = ±1.5). `12x − Ry = −18` gives
`y = (12x + 18)/R`, which equals `6x + 9` uniquely at R = 2 — positive, as the stem requires.
Worth confirming against another copy of this form if one turns up.
