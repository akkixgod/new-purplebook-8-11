## Q3 — `math2-q03-capacitor-graph.svg` (page 78)

First-quadrant exponential decay with context axis titles.

- **Plot extent**: x from 0 to 4.35, y from 0 to 21.4
- **Grid**: thin `#c9ccd1` verticals every 0.5 and horizontals every 2
- **x-axis**: ticks 1–4; italic `O`; arrowhead and italic `x`; title *Time (seconds)*
- **y-axis**: ticks 2, 4, …, 20; arrowhead and italic `y`; rotated title *Potential difference (volts)*
- **Curve**: `V(t) = 16(1/3)^t` through `(0, 16)`, about `(1, 5.3)`, and `y = 2.4` near `t = 1.73`
- **Answer note**: 15% of 16 V is 2.4 V → **1.71** (**C**). Distractors 0.43 / 0.85 / 3.41 are 1/4, 1/2, and 2× that time.
