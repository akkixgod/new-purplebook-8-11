## Q1 — `math1-q01-triangle.svg` (page 56)

- Isosceles triangle, vertex up. Base labeled italic **x**; both slanted sides

## Q12 — `math1-q12-similar-triangles.svg` (page 66)

- Right triangle **CAE** with right angles at **E** (base) and **D** (inner).
  C at the top of the vertical side, A bottom-left, E bottom-right. **B** on
  hypotenuse AC; **D** on CE; **BD ∥ AE**. Same layout as Int-E Math M1 Q10
  (that item used 56° / AE = 22(BD); this one is 57° / AE = 21(BD)).
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** corresponding angles, m∠CAE = m∠CBD = 57° → **C**.
  The length ratio AE = 21(BD) is unused.

## Q13 — `math1-q13-alkane-graph.svg` (page 67)

- **Axes:** first quadrant plus negative y. x from 0 to ~250 (labels every 50);
  y from −200 to 300 (labels every 50). Grid every 25. Italic `x` / `y`, italic
  `O` at the origin.
- **Curve:** increasing, slightly concave-down; starts near **(0, −175)**;
  crosses the x-axis near **x = 70**; marked filled point **(141.32, 172.26)**;
  leaves the top of the plot before x = 250.
- **Answer:** x = molecular weight, y = boiling point → **B**.

## Q17 — choice graphs, no SVG (page 70)

- Stem scrolled off the top of the capture. Reconstructed as the standard SAT
  zinnia/cosmos system: total 7 seeds, zinnias $2 / cosmos $1, spent $14, so
  x + y = 7 and 2x + y = 14.
- **B** (fully visible): y = 2x and x + y = 7 on a 0–10 by 0–14 grid.
- **C** (fully visible, selected by the test-taker): x + y = 7 and 2x + y = 14
  on a matching grid with y to 16. Axis labels: “Number of zinnia seeds” /
  “Number of cosmos seeds”.
- **A** and **D** reconstructed as the other two pairings of those constraints.
- **Answer:** C. Key follows from the reconstructed stem; B is the classic
  “$2 each → y = 2x” trap. If the missing stem were instead “twice as many
  cosmos as zinnias, total 7,” the key would flip to B.

## Q21 — `math1-q21-ramp.svg` (page 74)

- Right triangle with horizontal base **x**, vertical **height of the ramp**,
  hypotenuse **length of the ramp**, and **θ** at the ground angle. Right-angle
  mark at the far end of the base. Same layout as Int-E Math M1 Q18 (that item
  used height 22.7; this one uses 29.1).
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** tan θ = 29.1/x ≤ 1/12 → x ≥ 349.2.

## Q22 — `math1-q22-exponential.svg` (page 75)

- **Axes:** x from −4 to 4 (ticks/labels every 2), y from −6 to 10
  (ticks/labels every 2). Unit grid. Italic `x` / `y`, italic `O` at the origin.
- **Curve:** **y = 5^x − 4**. Horizontal asymptote **y = −4** from the left;
  filled dots at **(0, −3)** and **(1, 1)**; leaves the top near x ≈ 1.66.
- **Answer:** a = 5, b = −4, so a − b = 9.
