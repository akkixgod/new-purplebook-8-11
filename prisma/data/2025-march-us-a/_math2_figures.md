## Q10 — `math2-q10-line-graph.svg` (page 92)

First-quadrant distance–time line. Pixel-traced from page-92.png: y-axis at
x ≈ 1196, x-axis at y ≈ 603, right end of x-axis ≈ 1514, top of y-axis ≈ 276
(900 m), so ~32 px per x-unit and ~0.36 px per y-unit.

- **Plot extent**: x from 0 to 10.8, y from 0 to 960
- **Grid**: thin `#c9ccd1` verticals every 1 and horizontals every 50
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels 1 through 10; italic `O` just below-right of the
  origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels 100, 200, …, 900; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Line**: 2.4 px black from `(0, 50)` (halfway to the first 100-grid) to
  `(10, 600)` (on the 6th labelled horizontal)
- **Marked points**: filled black dots at both endpoints
- **Answer note**: y is *total* distance on the path. Distance walked **in** the
  10 minutes after leaving the fountain is `600 − 50 = 550`, not the y-value 600
  at x = 10. The on-screen grid-in `600` is the trap, not the key.

int-d Q13 (`−36`).

- **Grid**: thin `#c9ccd1` lines every 1 unit
- **x-axis**: along y = 0 (top of the plot); ticks −10, −8, −6, −4, −2; italic
  `O`; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right

  of the axis; arrowhead and italic `y` on top

- **Marked points**: `(0, −13)` and `(−5, −12)`
- **Shaded region**: everything **above** the line (`x + 5y ≥ −65`)
- **Answer note**: r = 1, t = 5 → **r + t = 6** (**A**)

## Q14 — `math2-q14-salmon-histogram.svg` (page 89)

Closed rectangular plot with contiguous gray bars, L-axis labels, and
horizontal grid at every integer frequency.

- **viewBox** `0 0 520 360`
- **y-axis**: 0 to 6, label *Number of salmon* (rotated)
- **x-axis**: ticks 70, 75, 80, 85, 90, 95; label *Weight (pounds)*
- **Bars** (left to right): heights **2, 1, 2, 6, 3**
- **Fill**: `#c4c4c4` with black stroke; no gap between bins
- **Answer note**: adding 32 lb (below every original value ≥ 70) must decrease
  the mean. The median stays in the 85–90 bin, so it need not decrease →
  **II only** (**B**)
