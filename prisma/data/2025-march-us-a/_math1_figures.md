## Q2 — `math1-q02-exponential-graph.svg` (page 56)

- **Axes:** x from −10 to 10 (ticks/labels every 2), y from −2 to 14
  (ticks/labels every 2). Unit grid (`#c9ccd1`). Italic `x` / `y`, italic
  `O` at the origin.
- **Curve:** **y = 3·2^x + 6**. Horizontal asymptote **y = 6** from the
  left; passes through **(0, 9)**; leaves the top of the plot near x ≈ 1.42.
- **Answer:** y-intercept is (0, 9) → **D**. Choice C is the asymptote.

## Q5 — `math1-q05-right-triangle.svg` (page 58)

- Right triangle with the right-angle mark at the **bottom-left**. Vertical
  leg labeled **18**, horizontal leg labeled **40**, hypotenuse labeled
  italic **x**.
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** x^2 = 18^2 + 40^2 = 1,924 → x = √1,924 → **D**.
