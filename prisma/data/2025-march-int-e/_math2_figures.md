## Q2 — `math2-q02-scatterplot.svg` (page 78)

Scatterplot confined to the second quadrant, with a solid line of best fit.
Same item-family as 2025-march-int-a Math M1 Q8 (intercept 2.3 there, 1.5 here).

- **viewBox** follows `Plot(-14, 0, 0, 20, ux=22, uy=16.5)` with extra right pad
  for the y-labels
- **Plot extent**: x from −14 to 0, y from 0 to 20
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 2 units in both directions
- **x-axis**: along y = 0; ticks and labels at −14, −12, −10, −8, −6, −4, −2;
  italic `O` just below-left of the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: drawn at x = 0 (the right edge of the plot); ticks and labels 2, 4,
  …, 20 placed to the **right** of the axis; arrowhead and italic `y` on top
- **Line of best fit**: 2.4 px solid black, `y = −x + 1.5`, from `(−14, 15.5)`
  to `(0, 1.5)`
- **6 data points** (filled black circles, approximate positions from the
  screenshot): `(−14, 15)`, `(−12, 13.5)`, `(−10, 11.5)`, `(−8, 10)`,
  `(−6, 8.2)`, `(−4, 4.8)`
- **Answer note**: negative slope −1 with y-intercept ≈ 1.5 → **y = −x + 1.5** (**D**)

## Q9 — `math2-q09-exponential-graph.svg` (page 85)

Reflected exponential falling ever more steeply as x increases, lying entirely
below the horizontal asymptote `y = −4` and never meeting the x-axis.

- **viewBox** `Plot(-10, 10, -10, 4, ux=22)`
- **Plot extent**: x from −10 to 10, y from −10 to 4; **22 px per unit**
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; italic `O`
  below-left of the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −10, −8, −6, −4, −2, 2, 4; arrowhead and
  italic `y` on top
- **Curve**: 2.6 px black graph of `y = −(5/2)^x − 4`, sampled every 0.04 in x
  - nearly flat just below the asymptote `y = −4` for x ≪ 0
  - passes through `(0, −5)`
  - passes through `(1, −6.5)`
  - clipped where it leaves the bottom of the plot at `x = log_{2.5} 6 ≈ 1.80`
  - no asymptote line is drawn and no points are dotted in the original
- **Answer note**: the whole graph is below `y = −4`, so it never hits the
  x-axis → **Zero** (**D**)

## Q13 — `math2-q13-intersecting-triangles.svg` (page 89)

Two triangles on opposite sides of a crossing, with equal marked angles.

- **viewBox** `0 0 500 310`
- **Line WZ**: from `W(90, 50)` through `Q(235, 145)` to `Z(380, 240)`
- **Line XY**: from `X(70, 230)` through `Q` to `Y(400, 60)`
- **Closing sides**: `WX` (left triangle) and `YZ` (right triangle)
- **Angle marks**: small arcs at `W` and at `Y`, each labeled italic `a°`
- **Vertex labels**: italic `W`, `X`, `Q`, `Y`, `Z`
- Caption centered below: *Note: Figure not drawn to scale.*
- Lengths are **not** to scale (WQ = 90 vs YQ = 27 would make the right
  triangle tiny). The original screenshot is likewise not to scale.
- **Answer note**: ΔWQX ~ ΔYQZ by AA, so YZ = 60 × 27/90 = **18** (**A**)

## Q18 — `math2-q18-exponential-graph.svg` (page 94)

Reflected exponential falling steeply just past x = 1. Same item-family as
2025-march-int-d Math M2 Q15 (base 6) and 2025-march-int-c Math M2 Q16 (base 3).

- **viewBox** `Plot(-2, 6, -5, 11, ux=52, uy=24)`
- **Plot extent**: x from −2 to 6, y from −5 to 11
- **Grid**: thin `#e5e7eb` lines every 1 unit in both directions
- **x-axis**: ticks and labels at −1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5; italic `O` below-left of
  the origin; arrowhead and italic `x` to the right
- **y-axis**: ticks and labels at −4, −2, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10; arrowhead and italic
  `y` on top
- **Curve**: 2.6 px black graph of `y = −4^x + 7`, sampled every 0.02 in x
  - nearly flat under the asymptote `y = 7` for x < 0
  - passes through `(0, 6)`
  - passes through `(1, 3)`
  - clipped where it leaves the bottom of the plot at `x = log₄ 12 ≈ 1.79`
  - no asymptote line is drawn and no points are dotted in the original
- **Answer note**: graphed curve is `y = f(x) + 2 = −4^x + 7` → `f(x) = −4^x + 5` (**B**)
