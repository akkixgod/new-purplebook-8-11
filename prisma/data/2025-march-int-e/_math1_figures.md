## Q1 — `math1-q01-cost-line.svg` (page 55)

- **Axes:** first quadrant. x (rings) from 0 to 100, labels every 10.
  y (dollars) from 0 to 300, labels every 50. Gridlines every 10 in x and
  every 25 in y (square cells: 10 x-units = 25 y-units). Italic `x` / `y`,
  italic `O` at the origin.
- **Line:** **y = 1.5x + 100**, drawn from (0, 100) through (50, 175) to
  the right edge (passes (100, 250) on the source).
- **Answer:** at x = 50 the line is at y = 175 → **C**.

## Q3 — `math1-q03-exponential-graph.svg` (page 57)

- **Axes:** x from −10 to 10 (ticks/labels every 2), y from −2 to 14
  (ticks/labels every 2). Italic `x` / `y`, italic `O` at the origin.
- **Curve:** **y = 5·2^x**. Horizontal asymptote y = 0 from the left;
  passes through **(0, 5)** and **(1, 10)**; leaves the top of the plot
  near x ≈ 1.53.
- **Answer:** y-intercept is (0, 5) → **D**.

## Q10 — `math1-q10-similar-triangles.svg` (page 64)

- Right triangle **CAE** with right angles at **E** (base) and **D**
  (inner). C at the top of the vertical side, A bottom-left, E
  bottom-right. **B** on hypotenuse AC; **D** on CE; **BD ∥ AE**.
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** corresponding angles, m∠CAE = m∠CBD = 56° → **B**.
  The length ratio AE = 22(BD) is unused.

## Q12 — choice tables, no SVG (page 66)

- Stem `f(x) = 47` with four tables of (x, f(x)) at x = 0, 1, 2.
- **A** and **B** are fully visible (all 0s vs all 47s).
- **C** and **D** were clipped. Reconstructed from the parallel
  constant-function items on Int-A (`f(x) = 29`) and Int-C (`f(x) = 22`):
  C = 0 / 47 / 94, D = 47 / 0 / −47.
- **Answer:** B (the only constant table). Key is unaffected.

## Q18 — `math1-q18-ramp.svg` (page 72)

- Right triangle with horizontal base **x**, vertical **height of the
  ramp**, hypotenuse **length of the ramp**, and **θ** at the ground
  angle. Right-angle mark at the far end of the base.
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** tan θ = 22.7/x ≤ 1/12 → x ≥ 272.4.

## Q19 — `math1-q19-vertical-angles.svg` (page 73)

- Two lines crossing in a flat X so the top/bottom pair is the obtuse
  vertical-angle pair. Labels **w°** (top) and **z°** (bottom).
- Note: Figure not drawn to scale.
- **Answer:** vertical angles, z = w = 112 → **D**.
